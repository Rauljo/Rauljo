## Selected work

### AI & machine learning research

**🔬 [HEPCoverageKG — Agentic GraphRAG for CERN literature](https://github.com/Rauljo/HEPCoverageKG)**  
My UCL MSc dissertation: a **provenance-aware knowledge graph** of ATLAS/CMS research papers, plus LLM agents that answer literature-coverage questions with **paper-level supporting quotations**. Built and evaluated both constrained-tool and free-SQL agents, and developed a Streamlit interface to inspect answers, evidence, and graph connections. The dissertation graph uses a **60-paper pilot**.  
`Python` · `LangGraph` · `SQLite` · `Neo4j` · `vLLM` · `Streamlit`

**🫀 [Cascaded U-Net — Whole-heart segmentation](https://github.com/Rauljo/Cascaded-U-Net-for-Whole-heart-segmentation)**  
A lightweight **two-stage 3D U-Net** to segment seven cardiac structures in CT/MRI scans. Reached **0.931 Dice (CT)** and **0.865 (MRI)** with approximately **7-second CPU inference**. Presented at **CARE @ MICCAI 2024** and [published in Springer proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-87009-5_16).  
`PyTorch` · `3D Computer Vision` · `Medical Imaging`

**🌍 [MuLE — Multilingual Language Equilibrium](https://github.com/Rauljo/MuLE)**  
Exploring multilingual reasoning alignment with **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)**. Developed a preference-pair generation pipeline for English, French, and Portuguese; used **QLoRA/Unsloth** to fine-tune a 1.5B reasoning model; and evaluated accuracy and language consistency on **MMATH/PolyMath**, including Spanish as an out-of-domain language.  
`Transformers` · `DPO` · `QLoRA` · `Unsloth` · `vLLM`

### Engineering beyond the lab

**🌊 [Maritime IoT — Monitoring mussel growth](https://github.com/Rauljo/IoT-Device-to-monitorize-mussels-growth)**  
A low-power sensing system for mussel farms, combining **LoRaWAN**, remote data collection, and a **Node-RED → InfluxDB → Grafana** monitoring pipeline. Includes hardware prototyping, a custom enclosure, and measured energy consumption. Recognized with the **Ícarus 2026 Prize** for socio-economic innovation.  
`ESP32` · `C++` · `LoRaWAN` · `MQTT` · `InfluxDB` · `Grafana`

**🧑‍💻 [Proyecto Batea — Mussel-farming operations platform](https://github.com/Rauljo/Proyectobatea)**  
A full-stack application for managing raft inventory, rope movements, and cultivation periods. Built with **authenticated APIs**, **PostgreSQL triggers** for inventory logic, alerts, and **automated weekly reports**. Tested in a real mussel-farming operation.  
`React` · `Express` · `Supabase/PostgreSQL` · `Cloudflare Pages` · `GitHub Actions`

**⚡ [CUDA MatMul Benchmarks — GPU performance](https://github.com/Rauljo/Cuda-Matmul-Benchmarks)**  
Implemented and benchmarked vector-addition and matrix-multiplication kernels on **NVIDIA T4/A100**, comparing custom CUDA implementations against **cuBLAS** and examining the cost of memory transfers and GPU execution.  
`CUDA` · `C++` · `cuBLAS` · `SLURM`
